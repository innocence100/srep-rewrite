**Status: documentation candidate prepared; final-candidate verification and
publication pending.** This record describes release preparation on the clean

## Release intent and boundaries

The 0.3.0 candidate is intended as the next official release, subject to
independent review and acceptance. The existing v0.2.1 GitHub prerelease
(release id `414169555`) remains a prerelease until separately promoted by the
release owner. This preparation does not change that release, its tag, status,
or any of its assets. Neither v0.3.0 tag creation nor GitHub publication is
performed or claimed here.

This candidate includes automatic resource-budget selection for `srep
compress` when the corresponding flags are omitted. Explicit `--memory` and
`--temp-limit` remain independent overrides; non-compress commands and the
library `ResourceConfig::default()` retain 256 MiB / 4 GiB. The policy is
best-effort, not a hard RSS or host/container limit. Known detector limitations
and behavior are documented in [`../releases/v0.3.0.md`](../releases/v0.3.0.md).

## Compression optimization lineage

The sparse-input m3 optimization goals accepted in 0.2.1 are present in this
0.3.0 source candidate unchanged. Specifically, the source retains pass-2
end-order fusion, compact authenticated candidate/DP rows, and the bounded
optional predecessor directory. The implementation, dependencies, and tests
are inherited from the previously reviewed source; this release preparation
does not modify production code or establish a new performance measurement.

The historical 0.2.1 comparison remains exactly what its original record says:
on its same-host frozen 512 KiB sparse sample-07 m3 comparison of
0.2.0-labelled binaries, archive bytes were identical, elapsed compression was
10,638.203 s versus 4,298.326 s, and managed temporary peak was
9,646,868,416 B versus 6,029,489,688 B. This is inherited development
evidence, not a measurement of the 0.2.1 package or a fresh 0.3.0 binary. The
measured peak exceeded the unchanged 4 GiB default; no 4-GiB pass or broader
performance claim follows. The original facts are retained, without rewriting,
in [`v0.2.1-publication.md`](v0.2.1-publication.md),
[`../evidence/post020-sparse-investigation.md`](post020-sparse-investigation.md),
and the 0.2.1 section of [`../../CHANGELOG.md`](../../CHANGELOG.md).

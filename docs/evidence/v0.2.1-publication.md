## Caveats and known limitations

- The release is a **limited prerelease**; `latest=false` and it is not the
  default "latest" release.
- The tag is unsigned (`verification.verified=false`, reason `unsigned`).
- `release-provenance.json` was hash-frozen **before** publication and is
  intentionally not rewritten: it still records `status:
  PREPUBLICATION_REVIEW_READY_NOT_PUBLISHED` with `published=false` and
  `tag_created=false`, because those fields were written before the tag and
  release existed and rewriting them would change the reviewed bundle hashes.
  The actual publication facts are recorded separately by the post-publication
  receipt described above, which independently verifies the published release;
  do not rewrite that frozen asset.
- The 512 KiB sparse sample 07 remains hours-level work for some methods. This
  is disclosed as a limitation; no future optimization is claimed here. Any
  0.3.0 automatic-resource-policy work is planned only and is not part of 0.2.1.
- The `README.md` and `CHANGELOG.md` embedded in each published archive are
  byte-frozen pre-publication snapshots at `6f1357fc`; the archives were not
  repacked. This repository's documentation is the current one.
- The published release carries the three native archives and the six evidence
  and checksum assets above; it does not publish CI workflow artifacts.
- Old releases are preserved unchanged: v0.1.0 (id `394791284`, 2 assets),
  v0.1.1 (id `399148285`, 5 assets), and v0.2.0 (id `408532238`, 10 assets)
  remain as they were; no tags were moved and no assets were replaced.
